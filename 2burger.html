This is the recipe to the chesse burger

  • Buy the best quality beef you can find and cook at 70 C to 60 C. 80/20 ground beef is juicy and rich, I don’t recommend choosing a leaner blend for burgers. Chuck is great, or if you want a little luxury in your life, a chuck/sirloin blend works wonderfully.
  • use brioche buns for some sweetest and also some squish to the buns.
  • seasonings: Garlic powder teaspoon, Worcestershire sauce teaspoon, mustard powder teaspoon, salt, and pepper add extra flavor to every bite.
  • Cheese: Use American cheese for a really classic, diner-style melty cheese.
  • Lettuce and tomato: Crispy, juicy and a must-have.
  • Caramelized onions: This topping is optional, but boy oh boy does it make that cheeseburger unforgettable. It makes a great hostess gift for summer BBQs, too.
